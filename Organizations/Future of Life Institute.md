# Future of Life Institute (FLI)

> Non-profit dedicated to reducing existential and large-scale risks from transformative technologies. Founded 2014. Best known publicly for the March 2023 "Pause Giant AI Experiments" open letter; also a long-standing operator of safety research grants, policy advocacy, and the Future of Life Awards.

## Founding and structure

Founded in 2014 by Max Tegmark (MIT cosmologist), Jaan Tallinn (Skype co-founder), Anthony Aguirre (UC Santa Cruz physicist), Meia Chita-Tegmark, and Viktoriya Krakovna (DeepMind). Operates as a US non-profit with affiliates and offices in the US and EU (Brussels).

affiliated-with:: [[Max Tegmark]]

## Mission and focus

Stated mission: "steering transformative technologies away from extreme, large-scale risks and toward benefiting life." Focus areas:

1. **AI x-risk and safety**: research grants, policy advocacy, public communication.
2. **Biotech**: pandemic preparedness, biosecurity policy.
3. **Nuclear**: focus on preventing nuclear catastrophe.
4. **Climate**: less prominent than the above three.

## Notable outputs

- **2015 Puerto Rico AI Safety conference**: an early significant convening of AI researchers on safety.
- **Research grants**: distributed millions in safety-research funding through several rounds (including Open Philanthropy-funded rounds 2015+).
- **2017 Asilomar AI Principles**: framework articulated at an FLI-organized conference, signed by hundreds of researchers and executives.
- **2023 Pause Letter**: "Pause Giant AI Experiments" open letter (March 2023), calling for six-month pause on training systems more powerful than GPT-4. Signed by Bengio, Musk, Wozniak, Russell, and thousands of others. Catalyzed substantial public debate about AI development pace.
- **Future of Life Award**: prize recognizing people who have prevented disaster (e.g., Stanislav Petrov, Vasili Arkhipov for nuclear near-misses).
- **EU AI Act advocacy**: significant policy presence in EU through Brussels office.

## Funding and influence

Funding combines philanthropic donors (Tallinn historically major), Open Philanthropy grants, and other foundations. Influence operates through:

- Convening events drawing researchers and policymakers.
- Research grants shaping fields.
- High-profile public communications (the 2023 letter being most prominent).
- EU policy advocacy.

## Reception and critique

- *From decel positions*: foundational; FLI's 2023 letter is treated as a milestone.
- *From mainstream AI*: mixed. The 2023 letter was criticized by some for vagueness ("Pause what, exactly? Enforced how?"), by others for being too cautious, by others for being too radical.
- *From FAccT critics*: critiqued for x-risk framing that critics argue absorbs attention from current harms.
- *From accelerationists*: paradigm opponent.
