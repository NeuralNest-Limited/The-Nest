## Positive-vision gap

Most futures work in the vault currently emphasizes risk. **Positive visions of human–AI coexistence are deliberately under-represented in global AI discourse**; this is a gap worth addressing in future curation. The Initial Coexistence Stance Draft is the only Synthesis note touching positive vision; see Curation Backlog for planned expansions.
