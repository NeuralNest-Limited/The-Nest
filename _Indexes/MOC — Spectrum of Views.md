# MOC — Spectrum of Views

> The vault's commitment to descriptive completeness made navigable. Every major perspective has its corner of the index here.

## How to use

Each section lists notes whose `perspective:` matches that label. Notes are typically position-summaries within debates, or position papers / books. Most descriptive notes use `perspective: neutral` and don't appear here.

## Decel / Pause

```dataview
LIST
FROM ""
WHERE perspective = "decel"
SORT file.name
```
