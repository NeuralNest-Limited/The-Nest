# Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services

> China's first dedicated national regulation on generative AI services, effective August 15, 2023. Part of a broader sequence of Chinese AI regulations (recommendation algorithms 2022, deep synthesis 2023, generative AI 2023) building China's most-developed comprehensive AI regulatory framework globally.

## Jurisdiction and authority

People's Republic of China. Issued jointly by the Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) and six co-issuing bodies including National Development and Reform Commission, Ministry of Education, Ministry of Science and Technology, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, Ministry of Public Security, and the National Radio and Television Administration.

Applies to provision of generative AI services to the Chinese public — domestic and foreign providers serving Chinese users.

## Status and timeline

- **July 13, 2023**: Adopted.
- **August 15, 2023**: Effective.
- Continues as live regulation; subject to ongoing supplementary measures and implementation guidance.

## Substantive provisions

Twenty-four articles covering:

### Content alignment with Chinese law and values (Article 4)
Services must adhere to the "core socialist values"; must not generate content inciting subversion, terrorism, separatism, or content contrary to public order. This is the most-discussed feature internationally — the regulation embeds content-policy requirements distinctive to Chinese governance.

### Training data requirements (Article 7)
- Data must be obtained legally and not infringe intellectual property.
- Data must include lawful sources; personal data requires consent or other legal basis.
- Training data must be of sufficient quality and accuracy.

### Algorithm transparency (Articles 9, 17)
Service providers must:
- Sign service agreements with users.
- Take measures to identify and reduce discriminatory content.
- Submit security assessments for services with "public opinion attributes or social mobilization capabilities."

### Labelling (Article 17)
Mandatory labeling of synthetic content generated by these services.

### Filing and security assessment
Algorithm filing with CAC and security assessment requirements similar to those under earlier 2022 recommendation algorithm rules.

### Enforcement

CAC enforcement. Penalties include orders to rectify, suspension of services, and administrative fines. In practice, services have been required to undergo security review prior to public release; some services have been pulled or modified.

## Reception

- *International policy comparison*: among the most comprehensive national-level frameworks at adoption.
- *Within China*: largely accepted; Chinese AI companies have built compliance capability into their development workflows.
- *Internationally*: dual reception — some elements (training data requirements, synthetic-content labeling) treated as potential models; content-alignment provisions treated as incompatible with non-Chinese free-expression frameworks.
- *From industry*: significant compliance cost; some companies have segmented Chinese and international versions of products.
