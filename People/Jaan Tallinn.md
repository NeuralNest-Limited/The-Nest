# Jaan Tallinn

> Estonian programmer, Skype and Kazaa co-founder, and the single most active individual donor in AI existential-risk philanthropy. Co-founder of FLI, founding board member of FHI / CSER / MIRI funding. Quiet but central infrastructure for the entire AI x-risk ecosystem.

## Background

Born 1972 in Tallinn, Estonia. Computer programmer; co-author of Kazaa (peer-to-peer file sharing) and co-founder of Skype (sold to eBay in 2005). The Skype windfall funded subsequent philanthropy.

Since the mid-2000s, has focused on existential-risk reduction.

## Contributions

### Philanthropic funding
Major founding funder of:
- Machine Intelligence Research Institute (MIRI)
- Future of Humanity Institute (Oxford, until dissolution 2024)
- Centre for the Study of Existential Risk (CSER, Cambridge — co-founder)
- Future of Life Institute (FLI — co-founder)
- Numerous other x-risk and AI-safety organizations

Tallinn's funding shaped which organizations exist; without him, the x-risk research ecosystem would look substantially different.

### Co-founding institutional roles
- **FLI co-founder (2014)** — see [[Future of Life Institute]]
- **CSER co-founder (2012)** — with Lord Martin Rees and Huw Price

### Public advocacy
Less publicly prominent than Tegmark or Bostrom, but periodic interviews and contributions. Distinctive tone: technical, understated, and very long-term.

## Positions

`perspective: cautious`

Tallinn is consistently cited as a "long-termist" — concerned primarily with civilization-scale risks over centuries-to-millennia time horizons. AI x-risk is the most-pressing current focus within that frame.

He has been publicly skeptical of timelines that frame AGI as either "decades away" (too far) or "next year" (too speculative); his own positions emphasize the wide error bars and the asymmetry of being wrong in either direction.
