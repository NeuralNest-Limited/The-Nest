# Holden Karnofsky

> Co-founder of GiveWell (effective-altruism charity evaluator) and Open Philanthropy (one of the largest philanthropic foundations focused on global health and AI safety). Among the most influential individual decision-makers on where AI-safety funding flows. Author of the "Most Important Century" essay series.

## Background

Co-founded GiveWell in 2007 with Elie Hassenfeld. GiveWell pioneered evidence-based evaluation of charity effectiveness. Open Philanthropy spun out from GiveWell (and the Open Philanthropy Project) and became one of the largest funders globally on cause-areas including global health, factory farming, and AI safety.

Karnofsky was Open Philanthropy's co-CEO until stepping back in 2023 to join Anthropic in a senior role. Currently at Anthropic.

## Contributions

### Open Philanthropy funding direction
Under Karnofsky's leadership, Open Philanthropy became the largest single funder of AI x-risk and safety research — funding MIRI, FHI, CHAI, CAIS, GovAI, Apollo Research, METR, and many others. The shape of contemporary AI safety institutional ecology owes substantially to Open Philanthropy's grant decisions.

### "Most Important Century" essay series (2021)
Long-form essay series at Cold Takes arguing that the 21st century may be uniquely consequential for the long-run future of civilization, with AI as the primary driver. The series became foundational reading for many in EA / safety communities.

### "PASTA" framing
Karnofsky's framing of "PASTA" (Process for Automating Scientific and Technological Advancement) as the key capability threshold for transformative AI — distinct from but related to AGI definitions.

coined-by:: [[PASTA]]

### Move to Anthropic
2023: stepped back from Open Philanthropy operations and joined Anthropic. The move was discussed as bringing safety-investment expertise into a frontier lab; some critics noted potential conflicts of interest given Open Philanthropy's prior investments in Anthropic.

## Positions

`perspective: cautious` / `safety-pragmatist`

Karnofsky's stated positions:
- Transformative AI within the 21st century is plausible enough to warrant substantial investment.
- Both alignment-technical and policy work matter; neither alone is sufficient.
- Uncertainty is high; epistemic humility is the appropriate stance.
- Effective philanthropy means concentrating on high-leverage interventions even under uncertainty.

He has been notably willing to update publicly — published essays explicitly track his evolving views over time.

## Reception

- *Within EA / safety community*: foundational figure for the modern funding ecology.
- *Within mainstream philanthropy*: respected; GiveWell's evidence-based evaluation model has influenced charity sector broadly.
- *Conflict-of-interest critiques*: Open Philanthropy's investments in Anthropic followed by Karnofsky's move to Anthropic prompted critique about funder/founder relationships in the safety community.
- *FAccT critique*: longtermist framing critiqued for centering speculative future over current harms.
