# Dario Amodei

> Physicist-turned-ML-researcher; CEO and co-founder of Anthropic. Architect of the "frontier-with-safety" strategy and the original drafter of the Responsible Scaling Policy framework.

## Background

PhD in computational neuroscience (Princeton, 2011). Postdoctoral work in neuroscience before transitioning to AI: Baidu (2014–2016) and Google Brain. Joined OpenAI in 2016, becoming VP of Research; led work on GPT-2, GPT-3, and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF). Departed OpenAI in early 2021 with a group of colleagues (including sister Daniela Amodei) to found Anthropic.

## Contributions

### RLHF and instruction-tuning
Led work at OpenAI applying reinforcement learning from human feedback to language models — the technique that produced InstructGPT and ChatGPT.

### Concrete Problems in AI Safety
First-author of the 2016 paper that legitimized AI safety as a research field within mainstream ML.

authored-by:: [[amodei-concrete-problems-2016]]

### Anthropic founding and direction
Set Anthropic's strategic direction: build frontier models to enable safety research, develop Constitutional AI and interpretability programmes, articulate Responsible Scaling Policy with capability thresholds (ASL levels) triggering pre-defined safety commitments.

defined-by:: [[Responsible Scaling Policy]]

### Public essays
"Machines of Loving Grace" (October 2024) — Amodei's articulation of an optimistic vision for AI's contribution to biology, mental health, economic development, and political institutions. Significant for framing a positive case from someone simultaneously committed to safety arguments. Notable for explicit attention to AI's *positive* potential rather than purely risk-mitigation framing.

## Positions

`perspective: safety-pragmatist`

Amodei's public position: AI development has profound benefits worth pursuing AND profound risks requiring active mitigation; the appropriate response is staying at the frontier with safety as a co-equal priority, not pausing. Has explicitly distanced Anthropic from both pure-doomer and pure-accelerationist framings.

His p(doom)-style estimates publicly range 10–25%, with explicit methodological humility about such numbers.

## Reception

- *Within Anthropic and adjacent safety community*: respected for combining technical capability with public articulation of safety arguments.
- *From decel critics*: critiqued as racing while claiming caution; Anthropic's commercial expansion under his leadership amplifies this.
- *From accelerationists*: critiqued as captured by safety arguments.
- *From civil society*: mixed; appreciation for transparency, concern about industry concentration.
