# Eliezer Yudkowsky

> Autodidact AI theorist and writer; founder of LessWrong and co-founder of MIRI. The most consistently public and most pessimistic mainstream voice on AI extinction risk; widely influential despite (or because of) operating outside academic credentialing systems.

## Background

Born 1979. No formal degree. Self-taught polymath spanning decision theory, probability, evolutionary biology, philosophy, and AI. Co-founded the Singularity Institute (later renamed MIRI) in 2000. Founded LessWrong in 2009 as a forum for rationalist thought; LessWrong has since become a major venue for AI safety discussion.

affiliated-with:: [[MIRI]]

## Contributions

### The Sequences (2006–2009)
Long-form essays published on Overcoming Bias and LessWrong, collected as *Rationality: From AI to Zombies*. Covers Bayesian probability, cognitive biases, decision theory, philosophy of language, and meta-rationality. Foundational text for the rationalist community.

### AI x-risk articulation
Yudkowsky's writing on AI x-risk, dating to early 2000s, predates and significantly influenced Bostrom's *Superintelligence* (which acknowledges Yudkowsky extensively). Key themes:
- Orthogonality thesis: intelligence and goals are independent — a superintelligence can have arbitrary goals.
- Instrumental convergence: most goals imply self-preservation, resource-acquisition, and goal-preservation as instrumental sub-goals.
- The hardness of value-loading: getting an AI to pursue human values is much harder than it appears.

coined-by:: [[Orthogonality Thesis]]
coined-by:: [[Coherent Extrapolated Volition]]

### AGI Ruin (2022) and the Shut It All Down position (2023)
"AGI Ruin: A List of Lethalities" (LessWrong, 2022): a comprehensive enumeration of reasons Yudkowsky thinks alignment is unlikely to succeed in time. Followed by his March 2023 TIME op-ed "Pausing AI Developments Isn't Enough. We Need to Shut it All Down" calling for international moratorium with enforcement (including military if needed) on frontier AI development.

### Fiction
*Harry Potter and the Methods of Rationality* (2010–2015) — extended rationalist-pedagogy fan-fiction; surprisingly widely read and credited with bringing many readers into AI safety / rationalist communities.

## Positions

`perspective: decel`

Yudkowsky holds an unusually high p(doom) — public statements have placed it at >90%. He has been explicit that this is a confident estimate, not Pascal's-mugging-style precaution. His position is that current trajectories lead to extinction with high probability and that nothing short of an international shut-down is adequate response.

proponent-of:: [[International AI Moratorium]]

## Reception

Extraordinarily polarized:

- *Within rationalist / MIRI orbit*: foundational figure; The Sequences are canonical.
- *From mainstream ML in 2010s*: largely dismissed as autodidact speculation.
- *From mainstream ML in 2020s*: substantial revision after capability progress matched some predictions; many former skeptics now treat his arguments more seriously.
- *From accelerationists*: paradigm opponent.
- *From AI ethics / FAccT*: critiqued for absorbing attention from current harms, for tone, and (in some critiques) for the rationalist community's epistemic monoculture.
- *Within decel*: even his allies often distance themselves from his most extreme tactical recommendations (military enforcement).
